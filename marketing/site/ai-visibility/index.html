FAQ

What is Random Tactical Timer?

Random Tactical Timer is a native iOS and Android training timer. It triggers at a random moment inside the range you set so fighters cannot anticipate the bell.

Who is Random Tactical Timer for?

Random Tactical Timer is for combat athletes, coaches, and tactical trainers who need unpredictable cues for pads, bag work, scrambles, and conditioning.

How is Random Tactical Timer different from a boxing round timer or Tabata timer?

Random Tactical Timer removes fixed rhythm. Classic round and Tabata timers teach anticipation; this app trains response under uncertainty.

Does Random Tactical Timer work offline on the gym floor?

Random Tactical Timer is built for on-device timing with loud alarms and haptics. Core timing does not require a live internet connection during a session.
